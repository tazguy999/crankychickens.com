How They Connect
Ng's L3 = Nick's Verified Process + Reliable Automation
You can't get to L3 without hitting ●●●● on Process first. Most teams try to jump straight from L1 to L3 and wonder why their automation breaks in production. Nick's framework explains why: they skipped Standardized.
hoppy-iterate is Ng's L4 with Process ●●●○
LLM as orchestrator, hitting ADO + GH + Notion. That's impressive technically. But the SOP lives in your head, not in documentation. Standardized but not Verified. The 2D grid says: reduce automation until process is fully verified, or accept the risk explicitly.
The graduation event is movement on BOTH axes simultaneously
Hatch's graduation system — nest score + flock score — is exactly this. Nest score = Process maturity. Flock score = Automation reliability. Graduation requires both bars full. Ng never says this explicitly. Nick built it into the system.
Nick's step 0 is the missing prerequisite to Ng's entire framework
Ng starts at L0. Nick starts earlier — at "do it manually until the pattern is visible." Without that, L0 is just guessing what to automate. With it, every subsequent level has a real SOP underneath it. That's the merge. Nick's step 0 is the foundation Ng's pyramid sits on.
